Danh mục — master data catalogs

Information architecture, landing, default detail shape, CSV import, consume-point drawer, bulk selection, and the Nhân sự master-detail variant. Working sketches; translate freely into Tailwind.

Before sketching: three reframes on the brief.

Reframe 1 — a flat sidebar of 20 catalogs is the actual IA problem. The brief fixes this on the landing with group tiles but leaves the sidebar alone everywhere else. I'd drop the sidebar entirely and replace it with two nav layers under the PageHeader: four group pills (always visible, color-tagged) + a strip of within-group tabs for the catalogs of the current group. An admin editing dịch vụ is mentally inside "Dịch vụ & Chuyên khoa" and probably wants to jump to loại dịch vụ or nhóm thuế next — not to cơ sở đối tác. The flat sidebar makes any-to-any jumps equally cheap, which is exactly the wrong optimization for a group-bound task.

Reframe 2 — "Lần sửa gần đây" and "Điểm sử dụng" are temporal halves of the same trust question. The brief treats them as separate features. But both serve admin confidence: what just changed? and what would break if I change this? I'd thread both onto every row (last-edited cell + usage-count cell) and merge them in one drawer with three tabs: Thông tin (edit), Lịch sử (row's own audit mini-trail with link out to full log), Điểm sử dụng. One drawer, three lenses on the same row. That also means mockups 2 and 4 are the same drawer seen in two tab-states.

Reframe 3 — "Điểm sử dụng" has two levels of consequence. "Used in 42 historical invoices" is safe-to-deprecate (mark inactive, historical records keep their snapshot). "Only tax group referenced by 15 active services" is a hard block — must reassign first. The drawer needs a verdict, not just raw counts: "Có thể ngưng hoạt động an toàn" vs. "Cần gán lại 15 dịch vụ trước khi ngưng." Counts alone don't tell the admin whether they're safe.

1. Landing

The tiles are the replacement for the sidebar, not a companion to it. Each tile is group-first with its sub-catalogs as individually clickable rows — the count on the right is half count, half deep-link affordance. The "Cập nhật gần nhất" line does double duty: it tells you who to ask if something looks wrong, and it's a click target into that catalog's audit view. Below the tiles, the recent-edits feed is a chain-wide view that crosses groups — this is where an admin who got a "hey, who disabled the gel supplier?" Slack ping starts their day.

2. Catalog detail — default shape

Four group pills sit directly under the PageHeader; the active group expands into a sub-catalog tab strip immediately below. This is the nav replacement — one cheap jump to any of the other 19 catalogs, but with group structure you can actually feel. The toolbar carries search, a status filter, a count readout that updates as you filter, and the primary CTA. Table rows show three attention columns that matter more than the default field dump: lần sửa (who/when), sử dụng (count with semantic coloring — amber means "used a lot, be careful," red means "referenced by active records, hard-block"). The drawer docks right when a row is selected; the brief asks for a drawer-not-modal precisely so the admin can cross-reference other rows while editing — preserved here.

Worth naming: the drawer has three tabs — Thông tin · Lịch sử · Điểm sử dụng — and this mockup shows Thông tin active. Mockup 4 shows the same drawer with Điểm sử dụng active. Same component, different lens.

3. CSV import — step 2, the dry-run

The three steps are nhận file → kiểm tra → xác nhận. Step 1 (drop + map) is mechanical; step 3 (confirm) is a one-click; the value and the design attention go to step 2, where the dry-run makes the whole flow trustworthy.

Three moves matter here. First, the three-number summary at top (N thêm mới / M cập nhật / K lỗi) is the promise: the admin should be able to read those three numbers and decide "commit" or "fix first" without scrolling. Second, errors are grouped by validation rule, not by row — 14 rows missing "Tên" is one group, not 14 rows to triage. Clicking the group expands to the row list. Third, errors are anchored to the source CSV line number so the admin can fix in their spreadsheet and re-upload without guessing which row is which. The "Tải xuống dòng lỗi" action exports just the error rows back as a CSV — fix → re-upload → done.

4. Điểm sử dụng — the same drawer, different tab

This is the same drawer from mockup 2 with the Điểm sử dụng tab active. That reuse is deliberate: a user who opens the drawer to edit can flip to "can I deprecate this?" without a different surface.

The top of the tab carries the verdict banner — the thing I mean by "counts alone don't tell the admin whether they're safe." Three verdict states: teal "Có thể ngưng hoạt động an toàn" (all references are historical, snapshots retained), amber "Có N điểm phụ thuộc đang hoạt động" (soft warning, can be fixed in-drawer), red "Cần gán lại trước khi xóa" (hard block, delete button disabled).

Below that, consume-points are split into two sections — Đang hoạt động (what's actively using this right now, must be reassigned) and Lịch sử (what has used it in the past, fine to leave alone). Each row is a deep-link: clicking jumps into that workspace with this row pre-filtered. The brief asks for counts; I'd pair every count with that deep-link, because "42 phiếu đăng ký" is less useful than "42 phiếu đăng ký → xem trong mục Đăng ký."

5. Bulk selection — the sticky action bar

The bulk bar appears only when selection exists; when it does, it replaces the toolbar's breathing room rather than floating above the table. Two reasons. First, a truly floating bar at the bottom of the viewport covers content on a laptop screen and the admin loses track of which rows are selected. Second, because the bar anchors to a docked area at the bottom of the table card, it scrolls with the page if the table is long — the admin always sees it and their selection context together.

The actions are bucketed by destructiveness: status toggles (Kích hoạt, Ngưng hoạt động) are cheap and reversible; export is neutral; delete is destructive and lives on the right with a color treatment that matches the verdict logic from mockup 4. Clicking Xóa on bulk selection triggers the same consume-point check as the drawer — if any selected row has active references, you get a "12 mục bị chặn" readout and the blocked ones stay unchecked after the batch.

6. Nhân sự — master-detail, not drawer

The right question for Nhân sự is: why is this catalog different from the other 19? It is because a user record has three things a generic row-drawer can't hold gracefully — an avatar upload, a signature image (shown inline, critical for ca-đọc sign-off), and fingerprint enrollment status. Each of those needs full editor width and spatial relationships (the signature preview is roughly 3:1 aspect; it doesn't fit in a 320px drawer). So Nhân sự gets the master-detail variant.

The coexistence with the generic shape is this: the same group pills and sub-catalog tabs run at top; the same toolbar + search is present; only the body below switches from (table + row-drawer) to (list + editor). From the admin's perspective the chrome is identical, only the work area expands. This is a pattern other catalogs could adopt if they grow nested fields — Bệnh nhân is a candidate if you ever want to surface patient's image assets here rather than in the patient workspace.

One detail worth naming: the fingerprint status on the right is not a form field but a device-interaction state card — it reflects what the fingerprint scanner currently knows. That's why it has its own card with "Ghi nhận lại" as the action, rather than being a text input. Pattern-wise, any field that requires hardware or external state belongs in its own card, not in the form grid.

7. Shared patterns worth preserving

Three components show up across every mockup and carry most of the IA weight. The group pills + sub-catalog tab strip replaces the flat sidebar on every detail view; it's the one nav component that makes 20 catalogs feel like 4×5 rather than 20. The row-context drawer with three tabs (Thông tin / Lịch sử / Điểm sử dụng) is the unified work surface — mockups 2 and 4 are that same drawer in two states. The verdict banner (teal / amber / red) appears on the Điểm sử dụng tab and on bulk-delete confirmation — a consistent way to convey "safe / reassign / blocked" whether the admin is acting on one row or forty.

8. Keyboard wiring

n opens a new-row drawer anywhere inside a catalog; / focuses the toolbar search; j / k move the selection cursor down/up through table rows (and are independent of which row has the drawer open, so an admin can audit-scan a table while keeping one row's drawer pinned); e opens the drawer for the cursor row; Esc closes the drawer but keeps the cursor position, so a quick spot-check on row 12 doesn't lose your place at row 8. On the landing, j / k walk the recent-edits feed; Enter opens the target catalog at that row.

9. States to think through before build

Empty catalog — "Chưa có dữ liệu" with a single + Thêm button and a secondary "Nhập CSV" link. Don't illustrate, don't onboard; an admin who reached this screen already knows what a catalog is.

Permission-scoped read-only — some catalogs may be view-only for non-admin roles. Row hover doesn't highlight, no drawer opens; the toolbar CTA is absent entirely rather than disabled-grey. Disabled buttons invite "how do I get this enabled?" questions; absent buttons answer that question silently.

Bulk action blocked on some rows — the delete attempt from mockup 5 hits the verdict gate and returns "3 mục đã xóa, 12 mục bị chặn — xem chi tiết →" with a link into a filtered view of the 12 blocked rows. Never silent partial success.

Long catalogs — Bệnh nhân has ~13k rows; the table needs server-side pagination with a readout like "đang xem 1–50 / 12.847" and a jump-to-row input rather than classic numbered pagination. Bệnh nhân might also deserve its own master-detail variant on the same justification as Nhân sự — if you ever surface images or attached docs at that level.

10. What I didn't draw and why

I didn't mock the CSV import step 1 and step 3 — step 1 is a file drop + header mapping matrix (the interesting part is step 2's dry-run I drew) and step 3 is a one-screen confirmation with a "Áp dụng" button that becomes the success toast linking to the audit entry. I also didn't draw the row-drawer's Lịch sử tab — it's the same shape as the recent-edits feed on the landing (actor circle, event text, diff hint, timestamp), just filtered to one row with a "Xem trong nhật ký đầy đủ →" out-link. And I didn't mock the "xóa có kiểm tra" confirmation dialog that appears when bulk-delete hits blocked rows — it's the verdict banner pattern reused, plus a "Giữ lại 12 mục bị chặn, xóa 3 mục an toàn" explicit action.
